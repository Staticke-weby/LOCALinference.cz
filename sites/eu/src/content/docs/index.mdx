## Where to start with local AI

<Aside type="tip" title="The minimum for daily work">
  **128 GB of unified memory** and **Qwen3.8 Flash Next**. Smaller
  machines are not in the catalogue — the model does not fit on them in
  usable quality with a sensible context. What does run on 128 GB is in
  the [device comparison](/devices/compare/).
</Aside>

</Aside>

Ran into a term you only half know? The [dictionary](/dictionary/)

## Why 128 GB is the minimum

Qwen3.8 Flash Next has 125 billion parameters and needs room for KV
cache. On 128 GB of unified memory it fits with a useful context; on
64 GB it does not fit in usable quality. That is why this site does
not cover smaller machines.

The trade-off is real: a 128GB box is not as fast as a data-centre GPU,
and not every model fits. What it buys is a machine on your desk, with
your data, and a predictable bill.

## An independent guide

We do not run our own benchmarks. Figures come from vendors,
independent labs and community reports, always with a source and a
date. Where sources disagree, we show the range and explain why.
