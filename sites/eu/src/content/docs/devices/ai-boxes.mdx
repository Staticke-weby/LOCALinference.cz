A desktop box is the default shape for local AI: it stays on, cools
better than a laptop and has room for 128 GB of unified memory.

## The four boxes

| Device | Stack | Bandwidth | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [DGX Spark](/devices/dgx-spark/) | CUDA, DGX OS | 273 GB/s | Integrated NVIDIA stack |
| [Strix Halo](/devices/strix-halo/) | ROCm, Windows/Linux | 256 GB/s | Lowest price |
| [RTX Spark mini](/devices/rtx-spark-mini/) | CUDA, Windows on Arm | 300 GB/s | Windows desktop |
| [Mac Studio](/devices/mac-studio/) | Metal, macOS | 614 GB/s | Quiet, efficient images |

## What they have in common

- **128 GB unified memory** — Qwen3.8 Flash Next fits with a useful
  context.
- **No discrete VRAM ceiling** — the model can use the whole pool.
- **One to four users** — more users need another box.

## What differs

- **CUDA vs ROCm vs Metal.** Tool support differs more than raw speed.
- **Cooling and noise.** A desktop stays quieter under sustained load.
- **Price.** Strix Halo is the cheapest; DGX Spark the most integrated.
