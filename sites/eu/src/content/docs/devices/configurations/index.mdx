The six device families have **25 OEM configurations**. They share the
same chip, memory and software; they differ in storage, chassis and
vendor. This page lists every variant.

<TabulkaKonfiguraciEn />

## How to read the variants

- **Memory is always 128 GB unified.** No variant has less.
- **Storage differs** between 1 TB and 4 TB. That is the main practical
  difference.
- **The chip is the same** within a family: GB10 for DGX Spark, Ryzen AI
  Max+ 395 for Strix Halo, N1X for RTX Spark notebook.
- **US list prices are not published per SKU.** This edition does not
  invent them or link to retailers.

## Which family?

- [DGX Spark](/devices/dgx-spark/) — CUDA, DGX OS, the most integrated
  stack.
- [Strix Halo](/devices/strix-halo/) — the cheapest route to 128 GB.
- [RTX Spark notebook](/devices/rtx-spark-notebook/) — 128 GB and CUDA
  in a laptop.
