A 128GB laptop puts the whole model in a bag. The trade-off is heat,
battery life and a smaller thermal envelope than a desktop.

## The two laptops

| Device | Stack | Bandwidth | Best for |
|---|---|---|---|
| [RTX Spark notebook](/devices/rtx-spark-notebook/) | CUDA, Windows on Arm | 300 GB/s | CUDA on the road |
| [MacBook Pro](/devices/macbook-pro/) | Metal, macOS | 614 GB/s | Silent images and audio |

## What to expect

- **Short runs are fine.** A 128GB laptop handles chat, code and image
  generation.
- **Long runs throttle.** Video and sustained agents are better on a
  desktop.
- **Windows on Arm is still a beta path** for some ComfyUI nodes.
- **Battery life suffers** under load; plan on mains power for serious
  work.

## What runs

Qwen3.8 Flash Next fits on both, with 128K tokens as the recommended
operating context. See the [model page](/models/qwen3-8-flash-next/).
