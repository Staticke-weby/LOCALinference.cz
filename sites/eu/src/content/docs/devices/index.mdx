The catalogue covers **turnkey devices with 128 GB or more of unified
memory**. No whiteboxes, no racks and no 24GB graphics cards: the model
has to fit on one machine, including KV cache and a useful context.

<TabulkaZarizeniEn />

## What the numbers mean

- **Memory** is shared between CPU and GPU. It is not a 24GB graphics
  card with a hard ceiling.
- **Bandwidth** sets the ceiling for token generation. More GB/s means
  faster decode, not faster prefill.
- **List price** is the vendor's US price. It excludes tax, shipping
  and local availability.

## What runs on them

The model this site is built around is **Qwen3.8 Flash Next**. It fits
on every device in the table at 4-bit or NVFP4, with a recommended
operating context of 128K tokens.

Some devices are faster than others. CUDA machines generally beat
ROCm and Metal on prefill; Apple Silicon is the quietest and most
efficient. The [model page](/models/qwen3-8-flash-next/) explains the
trade-offs, and the Czech edition has measured numbers for each device.

## Where the limits are

- **Long context** costs memory and speed. 128K is a working point,
  262K is the ceiling.
- **Concurrency** is limited by memory bandwidth. One box is for one to
  four users, not a department.
- **Availability** differs by country. This edition does not link to
  retailers.
