A guide that admits gaps is more trustworthy than one with a number for
everything. These are the main ones.

## Measurement gaps

- **RTX Spark mini** — no public Qwen3.8 measurement.
- **TTFT and prefill** — measured differently across reports.
- **Concurrency 4+** — most reports are single-stream.
- **128K and 262K** — speed and quality together are rare.
- **Noise and power** — few sustained-load measurements.

## Quality gaps

- **Czech tests** — no public benchmark at English level.
- **Vision** — documents and screenshots vary by task.
- **Tool calling** — benchmarks exist, operational tests are rare.
- **3D on DGX Spark and RTX Spark** — no public number.

## How we handle it

We mark missing data instead of guessing. When a figure appears later,
we add it with a source and a date.
