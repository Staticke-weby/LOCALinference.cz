We do not run our own benchmarks. Every number comes from a public
source, with a date and a configuration.

## What we record

- **Source type** — vendor, independent lab or community.
- **Configuration** — device, model, quantization, engine, context,
  concurrency.
- **Type** — measured or calculated.
- **Confidence** — high, medium or low.

## What we do with disagreements

When two sources differ, we show both and explain the likely cause:
quantization, engine version, context length, concurrency or cooling.
We do not pick the best number.

## What we never do

- Invent a number when no source exists.
- Present a calculation as a measurement.
- Compare figures measured under different conditions without saying so.
