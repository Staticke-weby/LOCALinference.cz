**Local AI** means the model runs on hardware you own. Your prompts and
documents do not leave the machine. You pay once for the hardware and
then for electricity, not per token.

## What it is good for

- **Sensitive data** — contracts, health records, client data, code.
- **Predictable cost** — one machine, no monthly token bill.
- **Offline work** — no network dependency.
- **Control** — you choose the model, quantization and context.

## What it is not

- **Not faster than a data centre.** A 128GB box is a desk-side machine.
- **Not unlimited.** The model must fit in memory, including KV cache.
- **Not maintenance-free.** Drivers, runtimes and models need updates.

## The minimum we cover

A **128GB device with unified memory** and **Qwen3.8 Flash Next**. Smaller
machines cannot hold the model with a useful context. See
[devices](/devices/) and the [model page](/models/qwen3-8-flash-next/).
