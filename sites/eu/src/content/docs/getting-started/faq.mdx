## Is 64 GB enough?

No. Qwen3.8 Flash Next needs about 100 GB for weights at 4-bit, plus
KV cache. 64 GB is not enough for usable quality.

## Can I use my own PC or a whitebox?

This site covers **turnkey devices with 128 GB or more**. Whiteboxes,
racks and 24GB graphics cards are outside the catalogue: the model does
not fit on one card.

## Which device is cheapest?

Strix Halo is the cheapest route to 128 GB. See [devices](/devices/).
Prices are US list prices, excluding tax and shipping.

## Is it as fast as ChatGPT?

No. A local 128GB box is a desk-side machine. Decode is tens of tokens
per second; cloud APIs are faster and shared.

## Does Qwen3.8 Flash Next speak Czech?

It is multilingual, but we do not have public Czech benchmarks. Do not
assume English-level quality for legal or medical text.

## Can I use outputs commercially?

It depends on the model license. Z-Image, FLUX.2 klein, Wan 2.2 and
TRELLIS.2 are safe; Qwen Research and Tencent Hunyuan are not. Qwen3.8
Flash Next uses the **Qwen Community License 1.0**: commercial use is
allowed, but offering it as a service or as a standalone AI work
assistant needs a separate license. See
[licensing](/generation/licensing/).
