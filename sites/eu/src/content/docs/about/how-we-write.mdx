We write for people and small teams considering their own AI, not for
ML engineers. The test is simple: after the first paragraph, a business
owner should know whether it matters and what it costs.

## Rules

- **Explain every term** on first use, in one sentence.
- **Business before technology** — tasks, time, money, risk.
- **No marketing** — no "revolution", no "game-changer".
- **No invented numbers** — a gap is marked, not filled.
- **Separate layers** — a short summary, then tables for detail.

## English edition

This is not a word-for-word translation. Titles and explanations are
written for an international reader; numbers, sources and caveats stay
the same.
