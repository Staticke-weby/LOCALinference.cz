We interpret public data. We do not run a lab. This page explains the
rules behind every number.

## Sources and dates

Every figure has a source and a date. Vendor figures, independent labs
and community reports are labelled. A number without a source does not
appear.

## Measured and calculated

- **Measured** — taken from a source, with device, model, quantization,
  engine, context and concurrency.
- **Calculated** — derived from documented inputs, with the formula
  shown. Never presented as a measurement.

## Ranges and disagreement

When sources disagree, we show the range and explain the likely cause.
We do not choose the best number.

## Roofline check

If a measurement exceeds the theoretical memory-bandwidth ceiling, we
mark it as suspicious or explain what made it faster (MTP, NPU, a
different quantization).

## Confidence

- **High** — two or more independent sources agree.
- **Medium** — one independent source or several community reports.
- **Low** — a single community or vendor figure.
