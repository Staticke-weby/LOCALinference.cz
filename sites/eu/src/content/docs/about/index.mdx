LOCALinference is an independent guide to **local AI on turnkey 128GB
devices**. It is not a news magazine and not a review lab. It collects
public measurements, normalises the conditions and explains what they
mean for daily work.

## What we do

- **We interpret public data.** Vendor benchmarks, independent labs and
  community reports, always with a source and a date.
- **We separate measured from calculated.** A number is either taken
  from a source or derived from documented inputs. It is never presented
  as our own test.
- **We publish the gaps.** When a figure does not exist, the page says
  so instead of guessing.
- **We show disagreements.** When two sources differ, we show the range
  and explain the likely cause.

## What we do not do

- We do not run our own benchmarks.
- We do not cover whiteboxes, racks or devices under 128 GB.
- We do not recommend models that do not fit on one machine.
- We do not use retailer links in this English edition.
