Legal practice is a natural fit for local AI: many documents, sensitive
data and repeated tasks. It is also an area where an error can have
serious consequences. The model is therefore not a replacement for a
lawyer; it is a tool for the first pass and for checking.

## Short answer

- **Summaries and extraction** are a safe start; **a legal conclusion**
  is not.
- **Data must not leave the office** — a local box is an advantage.
- **Every citation and clause** is checked against the source.
- **Confidentiality and liability** stay with the lawyer.

## Where it makes sense

| Task | What the model does | Risk |
|---|---|---|
| **Contract summary** | main points, parties, deadlines | a skipped passage |
| **Clause extraction** | notice, penalties, price | a wrong citation |
| **Due diligence** | an overview of documents | a false conclusion |
| **Research** | orientation in the text | an invented source |
| **Letter draft** | a first version | style and facts |
| **Annex check** | differences against a version | a missed change |

## Workflow

1. **Define the task** — summary, extraction, or a check.
2. **Choose the documents** and verify they are valid.
3. **The model reads and proposes** a structured output.
4. **The lawyer checks** citations and clauses.
5. **Work it into** the document.
6. **Record** the version and the source.

The model should not get an unlimited archive. RAG selects the relevant
parts and
[citations](/topics/rag/citations-and-evaluation/) show the source.

## Data and confidentiality

- **A local box** lowers the risk of transfer, but does not settle
  permissions.
- **Access** for specific people only.
- **Logs** can contain sensitive data; limit retention.
- **Backups** must be encrypted.
- **Deletion** must cover documents, the index and the logs.
- **The supplier** of the model and the hardware must have clear terms.

Professional secrecy is a duty of the lawyer, not of the software. A
local deployment removes the transfer to a third party; it does not
remove the duty to protect the file, and it does not make an
unauthorised reader harmless.

## Risks

- **Invented case law** — the model can cite a source that does not
  exist.
- **Outdated law** — the model does not know what changed.
- **Wrong context** — RAG selects the wrong document.
- **Faulty extraction** — an amount or a deadline.
- **Confidentiality** — a leak through logs or an agent.
- **Liability** — the model carries none.

## Example: checking an annex

A lawyer receives an original contract and an annex. The model compares
the articles, marks the changes and flags new obligations. The lawyer
checks the citations and decides what is acceptable. The model does not
replace the interpretation of law; it only shortens the first pass.

## What to measure

- **Time** to the first pass.
- **The number of errors** in facts and citations.
- **The number of documents** processed per hour.
- **How many outputs** passed without a correction.
- **The lawyer's satisfaction** with the first draft.

## How much time it saves

Legal work is full of first reading. An illustrative **calculation** for
due diligence with 200 documents:

| Phase | By hand | With the model |
|---|---|---|
| First pass, 200 documents | 15 min × 200 = 50 h | 3 min × 200 = 10 h |
| Citation check | included | 5 min × 200 = 16.7 h |
| Total | ~50 h | ~26.7 h |

The numbers are a **calculation** with sample inputs, not a
measurement. They show one thing: the model does not shorten the check,
it shortens the first pass. Anyone who skips the check saves nothing —
they only move the risk.

## How to introduce it

1. **Pick one matter type** — contract summaries, or annex checks.
2. **Take 20 documents** and write down by hand what the output should
   contain.
3. **Set up a structured output** — fields, not free text.
4. **Check 20 outputs** against the original and record the errors.
5. **Only then** let the model loose on a larger volume, still with a
   check.
6. **Turn on logging** — who, when, which document, which version.

A lawyer who cannot explain where the model took a citation from should
not use it. That is why
[RAG with citations](/topics/rag/citations-and-evaluation/) is a
condition, not an enhancement.

## Typical mistakes

| Mistake | Why it happens | How to avoid it |
|---|---|---|
| The model cites case law that does not exist | it has no access to a database | forbid free citations, verify |
| The summary drops an exception | long document, short output | output per article, not all at once |
| An old contract version | wrong index | metadata with version and validity |
| A wrong amount | OCR or transcription | validation against the document |
| A leak into the logs | the whole text is logged | log IDs and metadata, not content |

## When not to use it

- **A legal conclusion** without a check.
- **A court filing** without review.
- **Citations** without verification.
- **Data** you are not allowed to process at all.
- **Decisions** that affect people's rights.
