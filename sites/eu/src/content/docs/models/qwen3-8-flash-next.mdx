**Qwen3.8 Flash Next** is the model this catalogue is built around.
It is a sparse mixture-of-experts model: **180 billion parameters** in
total (125B main, 51B N-gram, 4B MTP), only **6 billion active per
token**. That is what makes it practical on a single 128GB machine.

## What it can do

- **Agentic coding:** SWE-bench Pro 62.5, DeepSWE 1.1 58.7,
  SWE-bench Multilingual 81.0.
- **Tool use:** Toolathlon Verified 73.5, CoWorkBench 73.9.
- **General knowledge:** MMLU 90.36 on the base model.
- **Vision:** text and images, including documents, charts and
  screenshots.
- **Languages:** multilingual, but quality varies by language and task.

Figures are from the model card and the Qwen blog; they are not our
measurements.

## Memory and context

At 4-bit or NVFP4 the model fits on a 128GB device with room for KV
cache. The native context is **262,144 tokens**; 128K is the
recommended operating point. Longer context costs memory and speed,
and quality on retrieval tasks can degrade.

The device has to hold the weights, the KV cache and the rest of the
system. That is why this site does not cover 64GB machines.

## Where it falls short

- **Czech and smaller languages:** no public benchmark we can point to;
  do not assume English-level quality.
- **Very long context:** 262K is a ceiling, not a working setting.
- **Concurrency:** one box is for one to four users, not a team of
  twenty.
- **Dense models:** they do not fit or run too slowly; this site treats
  them as a warning, not a recommendation.
