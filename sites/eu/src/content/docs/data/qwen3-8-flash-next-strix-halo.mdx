Strix Halo is the cheapest 128GB box. These are public community
measurements, not our tests.

## What the reports show

- **Decode:** roughly **36–63 tok/s** depending on engine, quantization
  and MTP.
- **Prefill:** **0.5–2.4k tok/s**, falling with prompt length.
- **TTFT:** at a 32K prompt, roughly **16–40 s** before the first token.
- **Long context:** 256K works, but decode drops and memory pressure
  rises.

## Configuration matters

Reports differ by engine (llama.cpp, Gufo, vLLM), quantization
(UD-Q4_K_XL, AWQ, EXL3), KV cache and MTP. A single number without that
context is not comparable.
