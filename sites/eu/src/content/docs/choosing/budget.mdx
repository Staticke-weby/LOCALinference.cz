At US list prices, turnkey 128GB devices start around **$3,500** and go
up to about **$7,000**. The model is free; the hardware is the cost.

## The range

| Device | US list price |
|---|---|
| Strix Halo | from about $3,500 |
| RTX Spark mini | about $5,999 |
| RTX Spark notebook | $5,900–$6,999 |
| DGX Spark | $6,950 |
| Mac Studio / MacBook Pro | not published in USD for 128 GB |

Prices exclude tax and shipping. This edition does not link to retailers.

## What the price includes

- The machine, memory and storage.
- The software stack: CUDA, ROCm or Metal.
- No per-token fees. Electricity and administration are extra.

## What it does not include

- **Tax and shipping.**
- **Electricity** — a few dollars a month at idle, more under load.
- **Your time** — drivers, updates and model maintenance.
