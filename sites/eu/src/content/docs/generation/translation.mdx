## What it is good for

- **Working translation** — emails, notes, documentation.
- **Subtitles** — speech transcription and translation.
- **Localization** — app and website strings.
- **Quick overview** — a foreign document before a human translator.

## Where it falls short

- **Specialist text** — law, medicine, technical standards.
- **Creative text** — style and humour are lost.
- **Czech** — English-first models have weaker word order and terminology.
- **Long text** — watch the context or the translation falls apart.
