For a small team or a solo developer, asset generation is the biggest
time saver: a concept, a variant, a texture. Not a finished asset — a
**starting point** that still needs work.

## What works today

- **Sprites and icons** — characters, items, buttons.
- **Textures** — surfaces, tiles, backgrounds.
- **UI elements** — frames, panels, icons.
- **3D props** — crates, barrels, furniture from a photo or sketch.

## What still needs manual work

- **Style consistency** — the model drifts between sprites.
- **Transparency** — backgrounds must be removed.
- **Scale and atlas** — sprites are packed into an atlas.
- **License** — the first check for a commercial game.
