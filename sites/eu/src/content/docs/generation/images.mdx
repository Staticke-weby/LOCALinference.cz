Images are the most mature part of generative AI. On a 128GB machine
you get a photoreal scene in seconds to tens of seconds and a poster
with text in a few tens of seconds.

## Measured times

| Device | Model | Settings | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| DGX Spark | Z-Image-Turbo | 1024², 8 steps | 7.5 s |
| DGX Spark | FLUX.2 klein 4B | 1024², 4 steps | 3.1 s |
| RTX Spark | FLUX.2 klein 4B | 1024², 20 steps | 17.75 s |
| Strix Halo | Z-Image-Turbo | 1024², 8 steps | ~24 s |
| MacBook Pro M5 Pro | Z-Image-Turbo | 1344×768, 8 steps | 22 s |

These are **sourced measurements**, not our tests. Conditions differ,
so the numbers are not a direct comparison.

## Where it falls short

- **Text in images** — short signs yes, small print no.
- **Hands and anatomy** — still fail sometimes.
- **Character consistency** — falls apart without references.
- **Czech text in images** — weaker than English.
