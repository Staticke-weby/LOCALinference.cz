Video is the heaviest workload. Expect **minutes per clip**, not
seconds. A 128GB machine can do it, but it is batch work, not
interactive play.

## Measured times

| Device | Model and settings | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Strix Halo | Wan 2.2 T2V A14B, 720p, 41 frames, 4 steps | ~33 min |
| Strix Halo | LTX2 T2V, 720p, 121 frames | ~10 min |
| Strix Halo | HunyuanVideo 1.5, 720p, 61 frames | ~15.5 min |
| RTX Spark | LTX-2.3 T2V 22B, 720p, 121 frames, NVFP4 | 97.6 s |
| MacBook Pro M5 Max | Wan 2.2 T2V A14B Q4, 512×320, 81 frames | 10 min 25 s |

Sourced measurements, not our tests. Resolutions and step counts differ.

## License warning

- **Wan 2.2** — Apache-2.0, commercial use.
- **LTX-2.5** — free under $10M annual revenue.
- **HunyuanVideo 1.5** — license excludes the EU; not usable in Czechia.

## Clip length

- **Five seconds** is the standard working clip.
- Longer videos are assembled from several shots, not one run.
- Sound is handled by LTX-2.5 and some other models; elsewhere audio is
  a separate step.

## Where it falls short

- **Character consistency** between shots.
- **Motion accuracy** — hands and objects fall apart.
- **Time** — a five-second clip can take minutes.
- **Heat** — long runs hit [throttling](/dictionary/operations/throttling/).
