The same model behaves differently on each platform. This section is
about the **environment**: what to install, how to verify the GPU and
what will not work.

## Four platforms, four worlds

| Platform | Install | Acceleration | What works | Limit |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Windows 11 x64** | Comfy Desktop or manual | CUDA | everything | drivers |
| **Windows 11 Arm** (RTX Spark) | Comfy Desktop ARM64 | CUDA | images and editing | missing ARM64 packages |
| **macOS** (Apple Silicon) | Comfy Desktop or manual | Metal | images, editing, audio | no CUDA, video via MLX |
| **DGX OS** (DGX Spark) | NVIDIA playbook | CUDA | everything | ARM64 and container limits |
| **Linux x86** (NVIDIA) | manual or container | CUDA | everything | distribution |
| **Linux** (Strix Halo) | manual or container | ROCm | images, video, 3D | kernel, GTT, FP8 |
