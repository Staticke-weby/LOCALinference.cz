You have a photo or an illustration and want a clip. This is
**image-to-video**, the most practical part of generative video: the
input image holds the composition, the model adds motion.

## What to expect

- **Short motion** — hair, water, leaves, camera. Not acting.
- **Scene preservation** — composition stays, details can drift.
- **Length** — typically 3–5 seconds; longer clips are a sequence of shots.

## On our devices

- **DGX Spark and RTX Spark** — CUDA, ComfyUI.
- **Strix Halo** — Wan 2.2 I2V at 720p, 41 frames: ~33.8 min.
- **Mac** — `mlx-video` or Draw Things. No public I2V number; T2V on an
  M5 Max takes 10.5 min at 512×320.

## Where it falls short

- **Faces** — blinking and mouth movement are the main source of errors.
- **Hands and objects** — can melt.
- **Text in the image** — motion breaks it.
- **Looping** — the model tends to return to the start.
