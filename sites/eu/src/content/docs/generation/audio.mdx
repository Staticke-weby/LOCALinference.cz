Audio is the cheapest part of generative AI. Small models run on a CPU
and take almost no memory on a 128GB machine. Unlike video or 3D, this
is seconds, not minutes.

## What works today

- **Narration** — long text in one voice, audiobooks, podcasts.
- **Voice cloning** — from a few seconds of reference audio.
- **Dubbing** — transcription, translation and a new voice.
- **Voice design** — create a voice from a description, no sample.

## License and ethics

- **Kokoro, VoxCPM2, Qwen3-TTS, Chatterbox, Ming-3.0** — commercial use.
- **Clone only voices you have permission to use.** Most licenses ban
  impersonation.
- Dubbing also involves rights to the original recording.

## Speed

We do not have public TTS measurements on our 128GB devices. The
VoxCPM2 vendor reports an RTF around **0.3 on an RTX 4090**, which is a
different device. Smaller models such as Kokoro run on a CPU; text
length, not the GPU, is the limit.

## Where it falls short

- **Emotion** — the model reads text, it does not act it.
- **Czech** — depends on the model; some have a noticeable accent.
- **Long text** — split into paragraphs or intonation drifts.
- **Names** — manual correction is often needed.
