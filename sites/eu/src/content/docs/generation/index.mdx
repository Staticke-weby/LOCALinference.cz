Beyond text, a local machine can generate **images, video, voice and 3D**.
It is not the same league as paid cloud services, but for drafts,
posters, illustrations, dubbing and game assets it is enough today.

This section answers one question: **what runs on a 128GB device, and
how long does it take.**

## What works locally

- **Images** — photoreal scenes, posters, typography.
- **Image editing** — removing objects, replacing backgrounds.
- **Video** — five-second clips, with or without sound.
- **Image to video** — a photo or illustration becomes a short clip.
- **Audio** — speech, voice cloning, podcasts, dubbing.
- **Translation** — text and subtitles, including Czech.
- **3D and game assets** — a textured model from a photo.

## Three things to know first

1. **License matters more than quality.** Some of the best models ban
   commercial use or exclude the EU. The tables have a Commercial column.
2. **Speed is not text speed.** An image is seconds; video is minutes.
   We never publish a number without device, resolution and steps.
3. **128 GB is enough for most, not all.** Models with 80 billion
   parameters are beyond a single box.
