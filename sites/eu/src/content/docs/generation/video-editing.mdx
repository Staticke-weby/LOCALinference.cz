Editing an existing video is harder than generating a new one. The model
must keep the rest of the shot unchanged — exactly what today's models
struggle with.

## What works today

- **Repainting part of a clip** (VACE) — mark an area, the model
  replaces it, the rest stays.
- **Extending the frame** — filling the edges, changing the aspect ratio.
- **Retake** — keep the clip and re-generate only a section.

## Where it falls short

- **Inconsistency** — lighting can change after a repaint.
- **Length** — models work in short windows; longer clips are assembled.
- **Audio** — the soundtrack is not edited with the picture.

For cutting and subtitles, a classic editor is still better. Generative
editing makes sense when you need to **add something**, not to cut.
