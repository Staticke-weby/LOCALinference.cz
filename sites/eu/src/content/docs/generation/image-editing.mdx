Generating an image is one thing. Editing an existing one — removing an
object, replacing a background, rewriting a sign — is often more useful.

## Three kinds of editing

- **Inpainting** — mark an area and the model repaints it.
- **Outpainting** — extend the image beyond its edge.
- **Instruction editing** — type "give her a blue shirt" and the model
  changes only that part.

## How it runs

On DGX Spark and RTX Spark, ComfyUI uses CUDA; on Strix Halo, Vulkan or
ROCm; on Mac, Draw Things. Editing is generally slower than generating
the same size, and we do not have public times for every device.

## Where it falls short

- **Hair, fingers, fine edges** — the most common failure.
- **Text in edits** — short signs yes, complex typography no.
- **Consistency** — a new background can change the lighting on the subject.
- **Speed** — editing is heavier than generating.
