A single photo becomes a textured 3D model. It is not a replacement for
a modeller, but for a prototype, a game prop or a product preview it is
enough today. On a 128GB machine this is minutes, not hours.

## Measured times

| Device | Model and settings | Time |
|---|---|---|
| Strix Halo | TRELLIS.2, 512, texture included | 116 s |
| Strix Halo | TRELLIS.2, 1024, texture included | 399 s |
| Mac with M4 Pro 24 GB | TRELLIS.2, 512, texture included | 5 min 13 s |

Sourced measurements, not our tests. Strix Halo is Windows with ROCm;
Mac is MPS. DGX Spark and RTX Spark have no public number.

## Format and quality

- **GLB** — geometry, materials and textures in one file.
- **PBR** — base colour, metallic, roughness; looks right under light.
- **Topology** — the output is not ready for animation; retopology is
  usually needed.
- **Scale** — GLB has no real-world dimensions; set them manually.

## License

- **TRELLIS.2** — MIT, commercial use.
- **Pixal3D** — MIT, commercial use; third-party components may differ.
- **Hunyuan3D 2.0** — Tencent community license excludes the EU.

## Where it falls short

- **The back of the object** — the model guesses.
- **Thin and transparent things** — wires, glass, fabric.
- **Topology** — not animation-ready.
- **Textures** — flatter than a real scan.
